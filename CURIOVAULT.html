About CurioVault '02

A personal knowledge archive

Inspired by the tactile charm of Microsoft Bookshelf, Encarta, and the Windows XP era, reimagined as your own niche-fact vault.

  • Create, edit and delete entries.
  • Organize facts into collections and search your archive.
  • Attach images and short videos.
  • Export or import a JSON backup.

Privacy note: entries and attachments stay in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). They are not automatically synced across devices or browsers. Export a backup before clearing browser data.
